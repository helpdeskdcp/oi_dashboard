⚡ Scalping Engine (fast-timeframe, own PAPER-TRADING track record)

NEW, un-backtested engine, separate from the swing-oriented signal engines above -- evaluates EVERY cycle (not just near a structural level) for fast premium-momentum ignition on real volume/OI/VWAP confirmation, with a tight volatility-scaled target/SL and a short {{ scalp_max_hold_minutes }}-minute max hold. Opens/tracks PAPER trades in its own separate bucket (never mixed with the S/R engine's win-rate) -- see the Scalping Engine card on the dashboard, or /api/scalp/<symbol>. NEVER places real orders. OFF by default until it's been watched for a while and the paper track record looks worth trusting.

Enable Scalping Engine (global)

☁️ Ichimoku Engine (trend-confirmation, own PAPER-TRADING track record)

Institutional Ichimoku Cloud engine (ichimoku_engine.py) computed from underlying candles -- feeds the New Trend Meter above as a regime-weighted trend-CONFIRMATION factor (disagreement with OI reduces confidence / can force WAIT, never silently averaged away) and runs its own separate in-memory paper-trading track record so it can be validated before it's ever trusted with real execution. ADVISORY/DISPLAY ONLY -- NEVER places real orders. ON by default; this is a kill-switch only.

Enable Ichimoku Engine (global)

🏛️ S/R Engine V3 (institutional-grade dynamic S/R, per-symbol, own PAPER-TRADING track record)

NEW, un-backtested engine -- fuses Price Action (Swing/VWAP/CPR/Pivot/Trend) with an ATM±3 Option-Chain OI-cluster read (Support/Resistance Strength, Institutional Wall Score, Fake Support/Resistance Index) and a previous-day validation (Valid/Weak/Failed) + current-day tracking (Held/Broke/Extended/Flipped) lifecycle into one weighted Confidence Score and trade decision. Opens/tracks PAPER trades in its own separate bucket (never mixed with any other engine's win-rate) -- see the S/R Engine V3 tab, the V3 card on the dashboard, or /api/v3/<symbol>. NEVER places real orders. OFF by default per-symbol until the paper track record looks worth trusting.
